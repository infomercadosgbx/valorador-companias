Operations and Supply Chain Logistics

Our operations, logistics, and customer service teams are at the heart of Amazon’s mission to be Earth’s most customer-centric company. Our dedicated teams deliver services and products that make life better and easier for millions of customers around the world every day.

Fulfillment & Robotics

Collaborative robots and new technology help sort and move billions of products and packages each year, improving employee safety across our operations network. These automation systems, along with advances in AI, help our employees as they deliver safely and quickly for customers. Many new categories of jobs now exist at Amazon because of the technology we've deployed at our sites.

Delivery Partners

We partner with a network of delivery businesses (Delivery Service Partners), independent contractors (Amazon Flex), and small business owners, like coffee shops or florists (Hub Delivery) to deliver millions of packages to our customers each day.

Amazon Supply Chain Services

Any business can now move, store, and deliver everything from raw materials to finished products using the same supply chain that supports Amazon and its independent selling partners.

Same-Day Delivery

Same-Day Delivery brings millions of eligible products to customers' doors within hours, from fresh groceries and prescriptions to everyday essentials, electronics and home goods. Customers can also choose 1-hour or 3-hour delivery windows on eligible items for even greater speed and convenience. We're continuing to expand fast, convenient delivery to more customers, including those in rural communities, helping them save time, skip trips to the store and get what they need when they need it.

Ultra-Fast Delivery: Prime Air

Prime Air is Amazon’s drone delivery service, bringing popular everyday items to customers’ doors in as fast as 30 minutes. Integrated into sites across Amazon’s delivery network, Prime Air offers ultrafast delivery on up to millions of items. The service operates under FAA authorization, including approval to fly Beyond Visual Line of Sight, enabling drones to safely serve more customers.

Ultra-Fast Delivery: Amazon Now

Amazon Now delivers thousands of products, including fresh groceries, household essentials, and locally relevant items, in around 30 minutes or less. By bringing products closer to customers, Amazon Now makes it easier to get last-minute necessities and unexpected needs delivered almost immediately, complementing Amazon’s Same-Day, 1-hour, 3-hour, and Prime Air delivery options.

Customer Service

We support customers around the globe, advocating for them through phone, email, and chat services 24/7. We also build world-class systems and innovative self-service solutions that are used by millions of customers each day on Amazon’s websites, shopping apps, and through Amazon’s family of digital products such as Kindle, Echo, and Alexa.
