What We Do

Across each of our businesses, we strive to make customers’ lives better and easier every day by relentlessly inventing on their behalf.

Stores

Selection, value, and convenience are the enduring customer experience we’ve always focused on to make Amazon the store customers turn to for their shopping needs.

Amazon Web Services

Amazon Web Services (AWS), the world’s most comprehensive and broadly adopted cloud, is guided by customer obsession, a rapid pace of innovation, commitment to operational excellence, and long-term thinking.

Artificial Intelligence (AI)

AI is transforming every customer experience and industry—and Amazon is building it into everything we do. From agentic AI that helps customers shop, plan, and get things done through Alexa+, to the foundation models and infrastructure powering hundreds of thousands of enterprise customers on AWS, to the AI-driven robotics optimizing our fulfillment network, we're focused on practical AI that works at scale.

Custom Chips

Amazon designs and builds custom chips that deliver industry-leading price-performance for AI workloads and cloud computing. From Trainium for AI training and inference to Graviton for general-purpose workloads, our chips power the next generation of innovation.

Devices and Alexa

We are focused on delivering a constellation of devices, services, and experiences that empower our customers to stay connected, safe, informed, productive, educated, and entertained. Our customers inspire us, and they’ve been at the heart of how we invent and evolve our products.

Entertainment

Amazon produces and delivers to customers world-class entertainment via Prime Video, Amazon MGM Studios, MGM+, Amazon Music, Audible, Wondery, Amazon Games, and Twitch. Amazon’s entertainment properties empower millions of customers around the world to enjoy a massive selection of the very best in movies, series, live events, sports, music, books, and podcasts.

Health Services

The mission of Amazon Health Services is to make it easier for customers to find, choose, and afford everything they need to get and stay healthy. We make healthcare more accessible and affordable by combining digital convenience with in-person care across Amazon One Medical, Amazon Pharmacy, and Health AI.

Operations and Supply Chain Logistics

Our operations, logistics, and customer service teams are at the heart of Amazon’s mission to be Earth’s most customer-centric company. Our dedicated teams create services and products that make life easier for millions of customers around the world every day.

Amazon ranks #3 on Fortune's World's Most Admired Companies list

In 2026, we ranked third on Fortune magazine’s Most Admired Companies list, marking our 10th consecutive year in the top three.
