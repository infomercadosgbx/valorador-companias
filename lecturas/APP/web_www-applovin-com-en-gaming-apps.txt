Common questions

Should I expect to grow my app further with ad-based ROAS campaigns?

expand_more

Yes. Ad-based ROAS campaigns help publishers scale by optimizing toward users who generate short and long-term ad revenue.

Can the impact be significant enough to change the trajectory of my business?

expand_more

For many studios, yes. AppLovin has helped games grow from early traction to global scale through compounding monetization and user acquisition growth loops.

What do I need to start running Ad ROAS campaigns with AppLovin Ads?

expand_more

You'll need a live app, Max integrated for monetization, an MMP, creative assets, and a defined campaign goal.

Can AppLovin Ads campaigns scale all types of games?

expand_more

Yes, whether your game is ad-monetized, IAP-driven, or a hybrid of both, AppLovin has a campaign type built for your business model.

Does AppLovin Ads cost money to use?

expand_more

There are no upfront platform fees to get started. You control your campaign budgets and only pay for the advertising spend you run through the platform.

Is my app a fit for AppLovin Ads?

expand_more

AppLovin supports a wide range of gaming apps. Before launching campaigns, apps are reviewed to ensure they meet our policies for demand partners .
