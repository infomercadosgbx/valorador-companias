Ads that fit your game

Max supports every major ad format, giving you the flexibility to monetize in ways that feel natural for your game and audience.

Rewarded Video

A fullscreen ad that users watch in exchange for an in-app reward.

App Open

An ad shown when the app is opened or when users return after using another app.

Interstitial

A fullscreen ad displayed at natural transition points, such as between screens or levels.

MREC

A 300×250px banner ad embedded directly within a game's UI, typically displayed in pause screens

Banner

A standard ad unit with text and image

Native

Native ads are fully customizable system-initiated ad units that appear as a seamless part of your app's design.

Common questions

Is Max free to use?

expand_more

Yes. Max mediation is free for publishers. Bidding networks pay AppLovin a fee on generated ad revenue.

Will my revenue go up when I switch to Max?

expand_more

Most publishers see a meaningful lift in eCPMs when switching to Max, driven by more demand sources competing simultaneously for every ad impression opportunity.

How long does it take to integrate Max?

expand_more

Most publishers can integrate Max in less than a day. Developer tools, including automated integration, SDK validation, and pre-launch ad testing with the Mediation Debugger make the process fast and straightforward.

I don’t run UA campaigns. Will Max still maximize my revenue?

expand_more

Yes. The 360 growth approach is available to Max publishers, but not required. Max’s unified auction maximizes the revenue per user regardless of whether you run UA campaigns.

Is the auction fair? Does AppLovin’s own demand get preferential treatment?

expand_more

Every network, including AppLovin, competes on equal footing in the unified auction. The highest bid always wins, regardless of the source. All bidding networks receive the same auction signals to bid competitively.

Is my app eligible for Max?

expand_more

Max supports apps across all major genres and categories. Please refer to AppLovin’s Publisher Policies to determine the eligibility of the app. Your account or support team can help determine eligibility.
