Item 5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

(b) and (e) On November 13, 2026 , Moderna, Inc. (the “Company”) entered into a Transitional Services and Retirement Agreement (the “Retirement Agreement”) with Shannon Thyme Klinger, the Company’s Chief Legal Officer, that sets forth the terms of Ms. Klinger’s retirement from the Company. Subject to the terms and conditions of the Retirement Agreement, Ms. Klinger will continue employment with the Company until March 31, 2027 (the “Retirement Date”), transition her duties as requested by the Company, and be deemed to have resigned from all officer positions with the Company on the earlier of the Retirement Date and such date as is designated by the Company. Until the Retirement Date, Ms. Klinger will continue to receive her current base salary and benefits and to vest in any outstanding equity awards. Ms. Klinger will be eligible to receive a bonus for 2026 performance based on her target bonus multiplied by the corporate performance factor determined by the Company’s board of directors.

Subject to the terms and conditions of the Retirement Agreement, Ms. Klinger will receive severance payments paid out in substantially equal biweekly installments over the twelve months following the Retirement Date in an aggregate amount of $1,628,300, representing twelve months of base salary plus her current annual target bonus. The Company will also pay Ms. Klinger’s COBRA premiums until September 30, 2028, subject to earlier termination if she becomes eligible for health coverage from a subsequent employer or ceases to be eligible for COBRA coverage. In addition, Ms. Klinger will be eligible to receive (i) $2,500,000 if the Company is the prevailing party upon the conclusion of all current and any related future litigation proceedings with Arbutus Biopharma Corporation and Genevant Sciences, GmbH and (ii) up to $5,500,000 subject to the result of the Company’s current and any related future litigation proceedings with Pfizer, Inc., BioNTech SE and related parties.

The Retirement Agreement includes releases of claims and certain other standard terms and conditions.

The foregoing summary of the Retirement Agreement does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Retirement Agreement, a copy of which is attached hereto as Exhibit 10.1 and incorporated herein by reference.
