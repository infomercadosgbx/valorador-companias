Item 1.01. Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

On November 9 , 2026 , Moderna, Inc. (the “Company”) entered into Amendment No. 1 (the “Amendment”) to its Credit and Guaranty Agreement, dated as of November 19, 2025 (the “Credit Agreement”), among the Company, as borrower, the guarantor subsidiaries from time to time party thereto, the lenders from time to time party thereto and Ares Capital Corporation, as administrative agent and collateral agent. The Amendment was entered into by the Company, Ares Capital Corporation and the lenders party thereto and became effective on November 9, 2026.

The Amendment reduces the applicable interest rate margins for the initial term loans and delayed draw term loans by 0.75 percentage points, from 5.50% to 4.75% per annum for loans bearing interest at a forward-looking term rate derived from the Secured Overnight Funding Rate and from 4.50% to 3.75% per annum for loans bearing interest based on the base rate. The Amendment also extends the maturity date for those loans from November 24, 2030 to November 24, 2031.

The Amendment extends the availability period for the first $400 million of delayed draw term loan commitments from November 24, 2027 to November 24, 2029 and for the remaining $500 million of delayed draw term loan commitments from November 24, 2028 to November 24, 2029, in each case, subject to earlier termination in accordance with the Credit Agreement. Borrowings of delayed draw term loans remain subject to the applicable conditions under the Credit Agreement. Except as modified by the Amendment, the Credit Agreement remains in effect, including its guarantees and collateral security.

The above summary of the Amendment does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Amendment, a copy of which is filed as Exhibit 10.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.
