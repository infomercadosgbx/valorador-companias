Section 5—Corporate Governance and Management

Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders

On November 18, 2026, Oracle Corporation (“Oracle”) held its 2026 Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”). Below is a brief description of each matter submitted to a vote at the Annual Meeting, as well as the final voting results with respect to each matter. For more information about these proposals, please refer to Oracle’s definitive proxy statement filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on September 25, 2026 .
