Innovation on Oracle Cloud

Department of War

The US Department of War (DoW) oversees the nation’s armed forces and defense operations. To streamline the acquisition of Oracle commercial software and services, the Department awarded Oracle a 10-year, single-award IDIQ contract under the Department of War Enterprise Software Initiative. The contract provides authorized DoW organizations—and government contractors acting under an approved Letter of Authorization—with a standardized ordering path for eligible Oracle products and services, supporting consistent terms, enterprise pricing, and more efficient procurement.

US Office of Personnel Management

The US Office of Personnel Management (OPM) leads workforce policy and human resources programs for federal agencies. OPM awarded Oracle the Federal HRIT Modernization Core Human Capital Management (HCM) contract to consolidate more than 100 outdated and duplicative federal HR systems into a single, secure, cloud-based platform. The effort is expected to improve HR data quality and processes, simplify employee and manager self-service, strengthen security, reduce complexity and duplicative spending, and give agencies a scalable platform for delivering consistent HR services to approximately 2 million US executive branch civilian employees.

Spotlight on AI innovators

Abjad

Abjad AI is a Saudi Arabia–based generative AI company developing Arabic-language AI solutions. To scale its generative AI workloads and advance its product roadmap, the existing Oracle Cloud Infrastructure customer expanded its OCI environment with additional GPU capacity. Abjad AI expects the additional capacity to improve performance and scalability and help accelerate the development of new products.

Applied Intuition

Applied Intuition is the leading physical AI developer for the automotive, defense, trucking, construction, mining and agriculture industries. Already an Oracle Cloud Infrastructure customer, the company recently expanded its OCI AI infrastructure footprint to support model training and inferencing. With OCI, Applied Intuition has improved performance and reduced costs to support its next phase of AI development.

Absci

Absci is a clinical-stage biotechnology company that combines AI-driven drug discovery with protein engineering and laboratory testing to design better biologics. To support the complex protein-design models and generative biology workloads running on its Origin-1 platform, the company expanded its Oracle Cloud Infrastructure environment with additional high performance CPU and GPU capacity. The expanded OCI environment enables Absci to run more AI-driven antibody designs and complex molecular simulations in parallel, helping accelerate biologics design while managing infrastructure costs as workloads grow.

Baseten

Baseten is the premier inference provider for the AI app layer and model labs. It makes serving AI models fast and scalable. As its customers expand globally, Baseten chose GPU infrastructure on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure across its global data centers to deliver low-latency inferencing for its customers’ most intensive AI workloads.

Cyera

Cyera provides an enterprise data and AI security platform that gives organizations the visibility and intelligent controls to protect data across cloud and on-premises environments. As the company grew, it needed cloud infrastructure that could both support increasing demand and meet regional data residency requirements. Cyera chose Oracle Cloud Infrastructure for its global reach, security, and performance. The move is expected to help the company continue to keep data close to where it resides, address regional requirements, and expand efficiently as demand grows.

Helm.ai

Helm.ai develops AI software and foundation models for autonomous driving and other physical AI applications. Automakers and technology suppliers use Helm.ai’s vision-first technology—spanning perception, path planning, and world-model-based simulation—to develop advanced driver-assistance and autonomous driving systems from L2+ through Level 4. As Helm.ai scales its production programs and expands its foundation model training, validation, simulation, and synthetic data workloads, the company entered a multiyear agreement with Oracle, selecting GPU capacity through OCI Compute and OCI Object Storage to support high-performance AI workloads with the flexibility to expand capacity as demand grows.

LiveKit

LiveKit provides a unified agentic platform for building humanlike AI that can see, hear, speak and act in real time. With demand growing for global, voice-first AI services, the company needed cloud infrastructure that could deliver strong performance, broad regional coverage, and favorable network economics, so it expanded its use of Oracle Cloud Infrastructure with Oracle Universal Credits for core services such as compute and networking. With OCI, LiveKit can support large-scale real-time communications workloads while helping control network costs and maintain strong margins.

Netris

Founded in 2018, Netris is a leading provider of network automation and multitenancy for AI infrastructure. Built and proven on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, Netris bring a public-cloud-grade networking experience, VPCs, isolation, and self-service to the most sophisticated AI clouds operating today.

NeuroSpark

NeuroSpark is an AI infrastructure system for running models on any silicon, turning infrastructure complexity into a unified system optimized for inference and training to achieve the best performance-to-price and outcomes for AI companies. NeuroSpark chose Oracle Cloud Infrastructure compute for its reliability, performance, and support across global regions.

Plaud

Plaud develops AI-powered note-taking software and devices that capture conversations and turn them into transcripts, summaries, and insights for professionals. With an expanding user base and growing inference workloads, the company needed compute capacity that could scale while maintaining high performance and low latency. Plaud chose GPU instances on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure Compute to support its inference workloads and help it deliver fast, responsive AI experiences as demand grows.

Rayzone

Rayzone Group provides cyberintelligence and cybersecurity solutions that use AI-powered data collection and analysis. The company chose Oracle Cloud Infrastructure as its cloud provider for critical workloads that require compute capacity and multiregion support. With OCI, Rayzone has improved analytics speed, reduced latency, and gained the infrastructure flexibility needed to support operational growth.

Stitch

Stitch provides an AI-native platform and professional services that help banks and fintechs build and scale financial products. To support growing transaction volumes, expansion into new markets, and increasingly demanding AI workloads while reducing infrastructure costs and administrative demands, the company selected Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, including OCI Database with PostgreSQL, which provide private, unshared GPU capacity. The OCI environment is expected to improve performance and reliability, simplify infrastructure management, help control cloud costs, and give Stitch the capacity to develop new AI-powered services and expand into additional countries.

Unconventional AI

Unconventional AI is an AI research lab developing next-generation accelerators for AI workloads. To speed up chip design and validation without fabricating every iteration, the company uses AI models and simulation tools running on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure GPUs and recently expanded its OCI environment with additional GPU capacity. Unconventional AI expects the additional capacity to improve the efficiency and performance of its simulation workloads while helping it advance new accelerator designs faster.

Oracle Cloud Infrastructure

It helps The Kroger Co., consumer packaged goods companies, agencies, publishers, and affiliated partners create more personalized and valuable experiences for shoppers across the path to purchase. The move is expected to reduce costs by approximately $1.6 million over three years, lower infrastructure risk, and give 84.51° a more flexible platform for its data and analytics workloads.

Aareon

Aareon provides ERP software and digital services to real estate and social housing organizations, including customers in France. To support growth, strengthen data protection and disaster recovery, and prepare for future cloud ERP services, Aareon selected Oracle Cloud Infrastructure to host its Oracle Database, Oracle WebLogic, Server, and related workloads in France. The OCI environment is expected to reduce hosting and administration demands, improve security and service continuity, and accelerate customer migrations while providing more predictable costs and support for future analytics and AI capabilities.

ACCIONA Energía

ACCIONA Energía is the world’s largest 100% renewable energy company with no fossil legacy. With 30 years of experience, operations in more than 22 countries, and 14.7 GW of renewable capacity, it provides tailored energy solutions to help corporate and institutional clients meet their decarbonization goals. After recently investing in two on-premises Oracle Exadata systems, the company wanted to move core database workloads to Oracle Cloud Infrastructure while retaining Oracle Exadata performance, availability, and operational continuity and improving long-term cost efficiency. ACCIONA Energía selected Oracle Exadata Database Service on Dedicated Infrastructure on OCI to support a cost-effective move to the cloud while preserving the database capabilities its teams rely on.

Affirm Software

Affirm Software is an Australian employment and HR workflow software provider with more than 20 years of experience and over 400 client organizations in Australia and internationally. To launch SuperVerify, its new solution for validating employee superannuation details during onboarding, Affirm Software chose Oracle Cloud Infrastructure for its flexible compute, scalable performance, built-in security and governance capabilities, and dedicated engineering support. The company is also migrating its broader platform to OCI to help reduce infrastructure costs and operational overhead, strengthen compliance for customers in regulated sectors, and support future growth through the Oracle ecosystem.

Australian Investment Exchange Limited

Australian Investment Exchange Limited (AUSIEX) is an Australian wholesale trading and investment infrastructure provider, offering trade execution, clearing and settlement services, online corporate actions, portfolio administration, and tax reporting across Australian and international equities, exchange-traded options, bonds, and cash. AUSIEX serves financial institutions, financial advisers, platforms, and wealth managers. AUSIEX is part of the NRI FinIT Group, NRI’s broader financial services ecosystem in Australia, spanning financial markets, investment operations, and technology. Backed by the global NRI Group, NRI combines more than 60 years of financial services and technology heritage with strong capabilities across consulting, quality engineering, managed services, and enterprise solutions in Australia and New Zealand. This combination enables NRI to bring deep industry understanding together with the technology expertise required to support complex, critical business initiatives. To move its established Oracle database workloads from Oracle Exadata Cloud@Customer to an AWS-aligned cloud model, AUSIEX needed a way to make the transition without replacing the Oracle AI Database technology its operations already rely on. AUSIEX selected Oracle AI Database@AWS with Oracle Exadata Database Service on Dedicated Infrastructure to retain Oracle AI Database and Exadata capabilities within its AWS strategy, minimize disruption to existing workloads, and support additional cloud use over time.

BCP

Banco de Crédito del Perú (BCP), a subsidiary of Credicorp Ltd., is Peru’s largest commercial bank and one of the leading financial institutions in Latin America. To strengthen critical financial processes, the bank modernized its financial information platform with support from Oracle Customer Success Services, using Oracle Database 19c, Oracle WebLogic Server, Oracle Analytics Server, and Oracle Financial Services Analytical Applications. As a result, BCP strengthened operational continuity, improved its ability to adapt to new business demands, reduced financial information access times, and enabled faster, more data-driven decision-making.

BH Telecom

BH Telecom is Bosnia and Herzegovina’s leading telecommunications provider, serving more than 2 million customers. A longtime Oracle Exadata user, the company selected Oracle Exadata Cloud@Customer and Oracle Exadata Database Machine to refresh its existing environment while maintaining technical continuity and minimizing disruption. The upgrade is expected to improve performance and manageability, reduce operating costs, and provide a database platform that can support BH Telecom’s current and future technology and digital initiatives.

Cyret Technologies

Cyret Technologies develops enterprise automation solutions, including DocMaven, which helps organizations automate document processing and administrative workflows across industries such as healthcare, manufacturing, retail, supply chain, and financial services. To support growing volumes of structured and unstructured documents while reducing infrastructure management demands, the company runs DocMaven on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, using its cloud, security, and storage services to support automated extraction, validation, and workflow processing. With DocMaven running on OCI, customers have reduced processing times by more than 80%, lowered operational costs by up to 50%, and achieved near-zero error rates, all while giving employees more time for higher-value work.

Deem

Founded in 2008, Deem Finance is a UAE financial services provider offering credit cards, personal loans, merchant financing, and corporate deposits to individuals and businesses. With OCI, Deem projects a 27% reduction in infrastructure costs, along with improved performance and disaster recovery, continued support for compliance requirements, and greater capacity for additional workloads and future AI initiatives.

To build and demonstrate multicloud capabilities for customers evaluating Oracle AI Database alongside AWS, Deloitte selected Oracle AI Database@AWS with Oracle Autonomous AI Transaction Processing for its development environment. The service enables Deloitte to develop real-world demonstrations and show customers how they can retain Oracle AI Database capabilities while using AWS, strengthening the organization’s ability to advise clients on their multicloud options.

GSISDG

The General Secretariat for Information Systems and Digital Governance (GSISDG) manages the Greek government’s IT infrastructure and digital services. To advance Greece’s technology priorities under the European Union’s digital agenda, the longtime Oracle customer expanded its environment to Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and Oracle AI Database@Azure. The migration has helped GSISDG improve performance, strengthen security and compliance, expand its hybrid cloud capabilities, and establish a platform for future AI and analytics initiatives.
