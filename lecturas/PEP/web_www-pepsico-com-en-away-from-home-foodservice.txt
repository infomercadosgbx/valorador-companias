Away From Home

PepsiCo Away from Home is a premier global foodservice partner leveraging the full PepsiCo portfolio to help customers shape the future of food and beverages where consumers work, dine and play. From local and national restaurants to recreation and entertainment venues, campus dining, healthcare, and vending, we deliver innovative food and beverage solutions designed to fuel growth and meet evolving consumer needs.

Become a Customer

With iconic, consumer-loved brands and powerful insights, PepsiCo helps elevate the guest experience while supporting your goals and driving sales growth for your business.

Our Away From Home Channels

Restaurants

No matter the cuisine, PepsiCo foods and drinks enhance every meal. From sandwiches to tacos and everything in between, PepsiCo offers trusted brands and partnerships support to help operators elevate their menus and delight every guest.

Recreation

With bold flavors guests love, PepsiCo helps power game days, live events and family outings. We partner with operators to elevate their foods experience, drive satisfaction, and create memories.

K-12

PepsiCo™ brings everyday favorites and fresh menu ideas to school dining. We partner with K-12 staff to deliver options that meet USDA child nutrition guidelines spark student interest, and enhance mealtime.

Learn More About K-12

Vending & Micromarts

With a wide selection of grab‑and‑go favorites, PepsiCo helps keep people satisfied wherever they are. We partner with operators to ensure vending locations stay stocked, convenient, and full of the choice’s consumers want throughout the day.

Healthcare

PepsiCo partners with hospitals, long-term care, and senior living operators to deliver trusted food and beverage options. Our dependable support helps keep cafeterias and common areas running smoothly for those in your care.
