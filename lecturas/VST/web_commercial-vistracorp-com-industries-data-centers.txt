Data Centers

Cool your servers at a competitive price and get solutions that help you use less and save.

AI is driving rapid innovation and constant change. But when it comes to the price of your power, we can deliver budget certainty.

Energy Solutions for Data Centers

Demand Response & Reduction Rewards

Conserving electricity can enhance efficiency while protecting uptime and operating margins. Through Demand Response (DR), your data center can earn revenue simply by reducing or shifting energy use when the grid is under stress, while maintaining full control over critical power requirements. Even in 24/7, mission‑critical environments, we help identify targeted, low‑risk opportunities to scale back without impacting availability, performance, or reliability.

Reduce load on your terms—without compromising uptime. Reduction Rewards allows data centers in ERCOT to earn incentives for scaling back energy use when demand rises across Texas. By responding when it makes sense for your operations, you can lower costs, support grid reliability, and maintain control over critical systems.

Energy-Efficiency Rebates

We have helped data centers complete major efficiency upgrades using our Greenback rebate dollars to make HVAC improvements and more. One data center was able to use these funds towards upgrades to its cooling systems on data floors, including new humidification controls, removal of redundant cooling fans, and extending chilled water storage time by six hours, resulting in savings of 1M kWh each month.

RECs and EFECs

Support enterprise-scale data center growth with the right energy attribute strategy. RECs help enterprise data center operators advance renewable energy goals and align with programs such as LEED, RE100, and EPA Green Power Partnership. EFECs offer a lower-cost path to zero-emission Scope 2 outcomes when carbon reduction is the primary objective.

Tailored Solutions to Keep Your Systems Running Cool 24/7

Power for Constant Uptime

Data centers operate around the clock—even when energy demand fluctuates. Vistra offers custom pricing structures and flexible products designed to efficiently support changing load requirements while keeping facilities and servers at consistent operating temperatures. Our experience serving critical load environments ensures reliability without compromising performance.

Market Insights for Smarter Energy Decisions

With deep experience supporting mission‑critical infrastructure, we understand the importance of monitoring market conditions. Our Energy Dashboard delivers timely insights to help you make informed, data‑driven decisions, while coincident peak services help conserve electricity and reduce costs during peak demand periods.

Incentives and Clean Energy to Support Growth

Lower operating costs and advance sustainability goals with solutions built for scale. Greenback rebate dollars provide cash incentives for installing energy‑efficient equipment, helping offset capital investments. At the same time, our renewable and zero‑emission energy solutions, including certified RECs, EFECs, wind, remote solar, and custom onsite solar, are helping data centers meet sustainability targets and support customer expectations.
